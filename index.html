About this Project

The Hindustani raag time system (Raag Samay Chakra) categorizes musical melodies (raags) based on the natural cycle of the 24-hour day. The day is divided into eight prahars (3-hour intervals), pairing specific raags with the corresponding emotional energy, light, and mood of that time. This project aims to provide the perfect Hindustani Music experience by providing curated performances matching your mood and the vibe.

About Raags

A Raag (or Raga) is a melodic framework used in Indian classical music. Derived from the Sanskrit word for "color" or "mood," a raag is essentially a specific sequence of musical notes. When played, it is designed to evoke a particular emotion or atmosphere in the listener's mind.

Here are some famous over-performed Raags, each button contains a special playlist with beautiful performances, listen and experience them.

Bhairavi

Known as the king of morning ragas, it represents majesty and deep devotion. Its signature swaying notes evoke a tranquil, meditative morning atmosphere

Ahir Bhairav

A beautiful fusion of Bhairav and Kafi scales, it features a highly melodic and slightly romantic feel, usually sung in the early morning

Bhimpalasi

A passionate and poignant raga often associated with the late afternoon. It evokes a feeling of separation and emotional yearning, resolving in a beautiful sense of hope

Yaman

Often considered the foundational "king of ragas" for beginners. Played just after sunset, it is universally loved for its majestic, pleasing, and deeply devotional mood

Bageshree

An immensely popular late-night raga named after the Goddess of Music (Saraswati). It is highly romantic, soulful, and portrays moods of deep longing and night-time serenity

Malkauns

A late-night raga that utilizes only five notes, giving it a mystical, somber, and deeply spiritual essence

Darbari Kanada

A majestic, grave, and heavy raga traditionally sung late at night. It is famous for its rich, vibrating, and deep melodic phrases

Bihag

Typically performed in the middle of the night, it is full of grace, romance, and joyful contemplation

Malhar

An ancient and powerful Hindustani classical raga deeply associated with the monsoon season
